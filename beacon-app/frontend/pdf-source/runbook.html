Anti-patterns · what NOT to say

  • Don't say: "It runs dbt show against the warehouse and authors a new model file." Say: "Ninety-two seconds from question to materialized answer."
  • Don't say: "Cortex Analyst." Cortex is intentionally absent from this demo. The story is build-time AI on the governed lake — the in-app feature is called dbt Wizard.
  • Don't say: "dbt Wizard generates SQL." Say: "dbt Wizard authors a governed asset — schema contract, tests, lineage, ownership."
  • Don't say: "Look at the typing animation." The animation is texture, not the value.
  • Don't open with the architecture diagram. Open with the Chief Risk Officer's question. Architecture is the closer.
  • Don't skip the business pages. After the wizard finishes, scroll through Markets, Surveillance, Risk & Clearing, Regulatory so the audience sees the persona surface that runs on the same gold layer.
